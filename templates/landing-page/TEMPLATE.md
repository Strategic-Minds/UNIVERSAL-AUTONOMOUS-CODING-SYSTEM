# TEMPLATE: Landing Page
## Type: Marketing/Campaign Landing Page
## Use Case: Product launches, lead gen, campaigns

### Sections (required order)
1. Hero (headline + sub + gold CTA + image)
2. Social Proof (logos or stats bar)
3. Features (3-column cards)
4. How It Works (4-step numbered)
5. Testimonials (3 quotes with stars)
6. Pricing (3 tiers: Good/Better/Best)
7. FAQ (6-8 Q&A, schema markup)
8. Final CTA (full-width banner)

### Design
- White background
- Gold CTAs (#f8b800)
- Clean typography (Inter)
- Mobile-first
- OG tags required
- robots.txt + sitemap.xml required

### Performance Targets
- PageSpeed Performance: ≥85
- SEO: ≥95
- Accessibility: ≥95
